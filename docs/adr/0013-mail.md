# ADR 0013: Mail — the status out of the house, and the weekly nudge

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-09-09

## Why now

A status that lives on a page nobody opens is a status nobody read. The
share link (ADR 0006, 0011) covers the people who click; the steering
group reads mail. And the person who writes the status forgets which
Thursday it is, which is the one habit the whole product rests on.

## The decision

One door for outgoing mail, `src/core/mail`: plain SMTP to a provider the
installation chooses (`SMTP_URL`, `MAIL_FROM`). Without it nothing is
sent, every feature that would send says so on the page, and the product
works exactly as before — dogma two: cut the internet and everything
essential still works. `MAIL_TRANSPORT=memory` keeps messages in a list,
which is what the tests and a developer without a mail account use.

Two things go out, and only these:

1. **The approved status.** Recipients are a list of addresses on the
   project, prefilled next week. At approval the project manager ticks
   "send to the recipients", or does not, every time; the tool never
   sends a status on its own (dogma five). The mail carries the summary
   and the manager's comment, the PDF rendered from the frozen report,
   and a link to the project for those with a login. The send is its own
   transaction after the approval, and its outcome is an event on the
   project, so an approval stands whether or not the provider answered.
2. **The weekly reminder.** One mail per member per workspace naming the
   projects with no approved status this ISO week, with a link to write
   one. Runs when a scheduler calls `scripts/remind-status.ts` or
   `POST /api/reminders/status` with `CRON_SECRET` as a bearer token; a
   Thursday morning is the intended time, and `docs/deploy.md` shows the
   cron line. Never twice for the same project in one week, because the
   send is recorded as an event. Demo workspaces and projects too new to
   judge are left alone.

## Boundaries

- The dependency is nodemailer, SMTP only; no provider SDK, no API key
  to any mail service in the code. The provider is whoever the
  installation points at, and for the hosted instance it must be EU
  (`docs/subprocessors.md`).
- Addresses are validated and de-duplicated at the boundary; at most
  twenty recipients per project.
- The mail body is the approved text. Nothing a participant wrote
  through a share link goes out verbatim in a mail; it went into the
  status the manager approved.

## Trade-offs accepted

SMTP is old and works everywhere, and it is also why delivery is the
provider's problem: there is no bounce handling, no open tracking, and a
wrong address fails silently at the far end. The reminder reaches every
member of the workspace rather than the project manager alone, because
a person on a project is not necessarily a member with an address — the
people table has an optional e-mail for later, and a per-person setting
would be the next step if members find the nudge too broad.
